A page that gets built while you scroll it.

Elements wait hidden until they scroll into view. Then a small crew of cursors builds them: boxes and images are dragged out with a live size readout, lines are drawn with the pen, repeated pieces are duplicated, and text is typed into place. Everything you end up with is the page's own markup.

FAQ

What happens if I jump straight to the bottom?

Anything you skipped past is finished instantly, without animating. Only what is on screen gets built in front of you.

What about content inside a closed panel like this one?

Hidden content is left alone. The planner only takes elements that are visible when it runs.

How is the text typed without changing the markup?

The text is already in place. The part not typed yet is made transparent with a CSS custom highlight, which shrinks as the cursor advances. No wrapper elements are added.
