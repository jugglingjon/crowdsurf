FAQ

What happens if I jump straight to the bottom?

Anything you skipped past is finished instantly, without animating. Only what is on screen gets built in front of you.

What about content inside a closed panel like this one?

Hidden content is left alone. The planner only takes elements that are visible when it runs.

Is the cursor animation here yet?

No. Phase 1 is the plumbing: deciding what to build, in which order, and when. The fade is a placeholder.
